# Ekow

Ekow is an intelligent platform for analyzing online reviews and comments. Using AI, Ekow collects and analyzes feedback left on social networks, e-commerce platforms and review sites, in order to generate detailed reports highlighting the strengths and weaknesses of a piece of content, a product or an establishment.

YouTube/TikTok videos, Instagram posts, Amazon products, TripAdvisor/Google/Booking reviews... Ekow centralizes the analysis so that creators, sellers and service providers can better understand their audience and their customers.

> **Project status**: Ekow is a proof of concept developed to an intermediate stage, now paused for lack of time. The online site serves as a showcase and a first preview, but the features are still to be implemented.

## Overview

Ekow targets three distinct user profiles, each with a specific need for feedback analysis:

- **Content creators** who want to track how their posts are received on social networks
- **E-commerce sellers** who want to quickly identify the weaknesses of their products from customer reviews
- **Service providers** (restaurants, hotels, local shops...) who want to manage their online reputation

The principle is common to all three offers: Ekow automatically collects the relevant reviews/comments, has them analyzed by an AI engine, then returns the results in the form of reports and actionable dashboards.

## Offers

### Creator offer
Optimize your performance on social networks with a complete analysis of your posts.

- Automatic tracking of your new posts or videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and more
- Overview of how your content is appreciated
- Detailed statistics on each post
- Detection of negative signals and bad buzz
- Comparison and tracking of your performance over time

### Seller offer
Optimize your sales by quickly identifying key feedback and weak points, without wasting time.

- Automatic analysis of customer reviews on your products (Amazon, Shopify, etc.)
- Detection and flagging of recurring problems
- Ranking of your best offers according to feedback and performance
- Overview of customer satisfaction
- Report on your best-sellers and purchasing trends

### Service offer
Master your local reputation and optimize the customer experience with an automatic, targeted analysis of online reviews.

- Customer satisfaction tracking
- Identification of strengths and recurring complaints
- Continuous analysis of Google, TripAdvisor, Booking reviews, etc.
- Comparison of your performance

*(common dashboard and many features under development for all three offers)*
